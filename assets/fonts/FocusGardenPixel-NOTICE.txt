Focus Garden Pixel is a modified regular-weight version of Pixelify Sans.
The digit 5 has a straight full-width top bar and a clear upper-left stem.
All other regular-weight glyph outlines and digit spacing are preserved.
The primary font family is renamed Focus Garden Pixel.
